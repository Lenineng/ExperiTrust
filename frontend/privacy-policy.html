EULA and Privacy Policy - ExperiTrust

Last updated: March 29, 2026

1. Data Collection and Use

ExperiTrust collects personal information including your name, email address, academic experiences, and job application history. This data is collected solely for the purpose of documenting, verifying, and presenting your experiences to potential employers. Your data will not be sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than what is stated in this policy.

2. Data Storage and Security

All personal data collected through ExperiTrust is stored in a secure MongoDB database. Access to this data is restricted to authorized personnel only through role-based access control. ExperiTrust takes all reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

3. User Consent and Rights

By registering on ExperiTrust, you consent to the collection and use of your personal data as described in this policy. You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw your consent and deactivate your account at any time without consequences

4. Verification Integrity

All experiences submitted on ExperiTrust are subject to admin verification before being made visible to employers. Submitting false or misleading information is strictly prohibited and may result in account suspension.

5. Third Party Access

Employer users can view verified student experience profiles on the platform. No personal data is shared with third parties outside the platform without the explicit consent of the user.
